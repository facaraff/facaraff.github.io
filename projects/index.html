projects

Funded research projects.

Cell network deterioration

Morgan Advanced Studies Institute (MASI) Summit project · 2023 · PI · £30,000

A framework for describing the behaviour and influence of single cells in multicellular populations, starting from single heart cells and moving to the features and interactions that emerge when they synchronise in a network. Collaborators: Prof Christopher George, Dr Hassan Eshkiki, Dr Nikol Sullo.

Lethal wilt in oil palm

Newton Fund, Royal Academy of Engineering · 2018 · CI · £50,000 · Partner: UNIPALMA

Land use in rural Colombia

GCRF competition 2018/2019 (DMU) · 2019 · PI · £23,500

Peace-process land in rural Colombia is again available for use but carries the legacy of war, such as improvised landmines and debris, which an AI-driven sensor array system is used to detect.
