My research is about computational intelligence and intelligent systems, with a strong focus on applied and fundamental research on heuristic optimisation.

## Heuristic optimisation

- Algorithmic design, hyper-heuristics and memetic computing
- Optimisation applications: customised meal optimisation, scheduling and dynamic clustering, hyperparameter optimisation, system tuning and calibration

## Algorithmic behaviour analysis

- Theoretical and empirical models of algorithm behaviour
- Search directions and disruptions
- Bound-constraint handling and infeasibility
- Structural bias, including the BIAS toolbox

## Applied AI

- Cell network deterioration: understanding and predicting disease, from single heart cells to synchronised multicellular networks (cell segmentation, detection and signalling)
- Sustainable farming and rural communities, including plant-disease early diagnosis and risk assessment
- Healthcare and medical sensor data
- Transitional climate change and its impact on businesses and agriculture
